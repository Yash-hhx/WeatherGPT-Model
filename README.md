# Weather Guardian AI

I want to create an application for my project WeatherGPT which is a conversational AI for Weather Forecasting, Alerts and Climate Information. It will give alerts for heat, rain, air pollution, thunderstorms, floods, cyclones and other natural disaster and alerts the people according to their occupations like for civilians, farmers, businessmen, commuters and concerned government authorities regarding what actions and precautions they should take in form of flashcards. Also it sends alerts directly to the people in the danger zone/disaster affected areas and concerned authorities(local + national) so they get to prepare themselves. It should also show the dimensions of the area which is under danger and show in form of a heatmap on a geographical map that what region is going to be affected the most and which region the least. Also an animation of the type of natural calamity which is forecasted should be shown in the background to make it more interactive.
